> **Critical**: You need to master ALL core topics, not just binary search

> **Focus Areas**: Arrays/Strings, Hashing, Linked Lists, Stacks/Queues,

---

## The Balanced Approach

**Binary Search is important, but it's ONE pattern of many.**

Your interview success depends on:

- ✅ Two Pointers (opposite ends, fast/slow)
- ✅ Sliding Window (fixed, variable)
- ✅ Binary Search
- ✅ **Linked List manipulation** (reversal, cycle detection, merging)
- ✅ **Tree traversal** (inorder, preorder, postorder, level-order)
- ✅ **DFS/BFS** (graph traversal, tree problems)
- ✅ Stack/Queue applications
- ✅ Hash Map/Set techniques

**Don't over-index on binary search. Balance is key.**

## Warning: Binary Search Obsession

**Don't do this:**

- ❌ Practice binary search 100 times in 6 weeks
- ❌ Spend 30% of kata time on one pattern
- ❌ Neglect trees and graphs for binary search
- ❌ Think binary search is the most important algorithm

**Do this instead:**

- ✅ Practice binary search 50-60 times in 6 weeks (plenty!)
- ✅ Spend equal time on each core pattern
- ✅ Focus on trees, linked lists, and graphs (more common!)
- ✅ Recognize that balance wins interviews

**Interview Reality:**

- You'll see 2-3 tree problems per loop
- You'll see 1-2 linked list problems per loop
- You'll see 1-2 graph problems per loop
- You'll see 0-1 binary search problems per loop (maybe!)
